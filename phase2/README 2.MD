Phase 2 – Visual Prompt Studio

This folder contains all work completed for Phase 2 of Visual Prompt Studio, including charcoal‑style and linocut‑style generated images, refined prompts, and both written and audio reflections. Phase 2 focuses on how different visual mediums shape texture, contrast, and emotional tone, and how a single concept can transform when interpreted through distinct artistic processes.

Overview

Assignment 2.1 explores the expressive qualities of charcoal — its softness, grain, and natural smudging. The charcoal image emphasizes:

• Soft gradients that mimic hand‑drawn shading
• Organic texture created through charcoal‑like grain
• High‑contrast focal points to guide the viewer’s eye
• Expressive smudging that adds movement and emotional depth


The reflection discusses how charcoal’s limitations and strengths influence composition, value, and atmosphere.

Overview

Assignment 2.2 focuses on the bold, carved aesthetic of linocut printmaking. The linocut image demonstrates:

• Sharp carved linework that imitates gouged marks
• High‑contrast black‑and‑white shapes typical of relief printing
• Intentional negative space used to define form
• Rhythmic repetition created through carved patterns


The reflection explains how linocut’s graphic style changes the emotional tone and visual storytelling compared to charcoal.



Reflection Summary

The Phase 2 reflections (audio + Studio2.1.md) examine:

• How each prompt transforms when interpreted through different mediums
• The relationship between texture and emotional impact
• How charcoal and linocut styles require different compositional strategies
• What artistic choices were intentional versus experimental
• How the two images relate to each other conceptually


This reflection shows strong awareness of medium‑specific design principles and thoughtful analysis of the creative process.
